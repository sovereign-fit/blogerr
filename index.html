2026 Florida Relocation Intelligence Brief

Understanding Florida 55+ CDD Bonds, Monthly HOA Fees & True Carrying Costs

Whether you are relocating from Pennsylvania (Chester County & the Northeast) or downsizing within Florida, looking only at the listing price and base HOA fee can hide $250 to $450+ per month in non-ad valorem CDD assessments and coastal windstorm insurance premiums.

1. Which Florida 55+ Communities Have Zero CDD Fees?

Four communities in our index carry zero CDD construction debt: On Top of the World (Ocala classic phases), original Sun City Center (Hillsborough County), Timber Pines (Spring Hill), and Valencia Grand (Boynton Beach). In The Villages, older homes in Districts 1–4 often have paid-off CDD bonds, whereas newer southern districts carry active 30-year bonds.

2. CDD Bond vs. Monthly HOA Fee Explained

Your monthly HOA or amenity fee pays for gated security, clubhouse recreation, fitness centers, and lawn care. A Community Development District (CDD) fee is billed annually on your county property tax bill and consists of two parts: a Debt Service Assessment (which can be paid off in a lump sum) and an ongoing Operations & Maintenance (O&M) Assessment.

3. Inland vs. Coastal Hurricane Insurance & VA Access

Central Florida corridors (Ocala, The Villages, Kissimmee, and Spring Hill) sit outside coastal high-velocity hurricane zones, saving retirees $2,400–$5,000+ annually on property insurance while keeping drive times to VA Outpatient Clinics and Medical Centers between 12 and 38 minutes.
